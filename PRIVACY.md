# Privacy Policy for HabitHive

**Effective Date:** November 2026

HabitHive ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a student portfolio project. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** Email address used for authentication (via Supabase Auth or Google SSO).
- **Habit Data:** Habit names, categories, completion logs, and streak metrics stored securely in Supabase and locally on your device.

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain the habit tracking service.
- To synchronize your habit logs across sessions and devices.
- To send reminder push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

## Data Security & Sharing
- We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
- Data is encrypted in transit and stored securely using Supabase backend infrastructure.
